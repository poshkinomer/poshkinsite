About Me

I'm Poshkin Omer — a student at Riverside Secondary in Port Coquitlam, BC, and one of the Co-Founders of Fraser Hack Club, the largest youth coding community in the Tri-Cities.

I believe coding should be for everyone. Through Fraser Hack Club, I've worked with School District 43 (SD43) to host coding events in schools — making programming more accessible for beginners who want to learn, and building a community for young people who code.

Outside of class and events, I'm building projects, learning new technologies, and working to make the Tri-Cities a better place for young developers. You can find our community at fraser.hackclub.com.
